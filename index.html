DAN Career Pathways

A student-facing exploration and job-search planning guide for Decision Analytics majors. Use this site to learn what DAN graduates commonly do, compare role families, test your interests, and build evidence over time. You do not have to pick one path and be done; the pathways below are highlighted because they reflect common DAN outcomes and useful starting points for career conversations.

Start here: how to navigate this Career Pathways site for maximum results
Use this page as a practical navigation map. The site works best when you move from outcome evidence, to pathway comparison, to employer and role research, to skill-building, and finally to a specific job-search plan you can discuss with your Career Coach.
Recommended student workflow

DAN Career Pathways navigation map

Do not treat the site as a one-time reading assignment. Work through it in a sequence: learn where students land, compare paths, identify employers and job titles, build evidence, and convert what you learn into a focused search plan.

Best use Complete one pass before a coaching appointment, then return to specific sections as your goals become clearer.
1 Review employment by function Use DAN outcome data to understand where Decision Analytics graduates most often land. 2 Explore common DAN pathways Compare finance, consulting, analytics, technology, marketing, and public-impact paths. 3 Build a target-employer list Use employer cards to identify organizations with historical hiring evidence and current-fit signals. 4 Translate interest into job titles Use job-title cards to learn the language employers use in postings and interviews. 5 Build proof for priority skills Use the Skill Builder and Curriculum Evidence sections to turn coursework and activities into evidence. 6 Create your job-search plan Convert your research into a target role, priority skills, outreach plan, and weekly search actions.
